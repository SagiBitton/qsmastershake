Common questions

How do I play? Look at the customer's order, tap the fruits in the same order, press BLEND and tap the customer to serve the shake. Tap the "?" in Settings to see the guide again.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app also deletes the progress.

Are there ads? There are no pop-up ads. A short video plays only when the player taps a green video button to get a bonus.

How do I turn off the sound? Open Settings (the gear) and move the Sound or Waves slider.
