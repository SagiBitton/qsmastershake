Privacy Policy

QSMaster Shake.io · Last updated: October 29, 2026

QSMaster Shake.io ("the game") is a smoothie-stand game for children and families, made by Manono & Play (Sagi Oz Bitton) ("we"). We care about children's privacy. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. It follows the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR and Google Play's Families Policy.

The short version

  • We do not ask for names, emails, photos, location or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no way to talk to other players.
  • Your game progress (stars, coins, cups, settings) is saved only on your device. We never see it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. All ad requests are marked as directed to children, so ads are not personalized and are rated for general audiences.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The game stores its progress in your device's local storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob only for optional reward videos that the player chooses to watch (for example, "watch a video to get a free gift" or a free spin). There are no pop-up ads, no banners and no ads between levels. AdMob is certified for Google Play's Families program. Every ad request from the game is tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and as under the age of consent (GDPR), with a maximum ad content rating of "G", and asks for non-personalized ads only.

To deliver and count ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address, device type, operating system, app version and ad interactions. Under child-directed treatment Google does not use this information for personalized advertising or remarketing. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

Some versions of the game may offer optional in-app purchases (such as coin packs or "No Ads"). Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive payment details. A grown-up check is shown before any purchase screen.

5. Children

The game is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information was collected through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Parents' rights

Parents can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data in the phone settings. Parents can contact us with any question or request at the address below.

7. Security

Because we do not collect personal information, none is stored on our servers. Ad traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, show a notice in the game.
